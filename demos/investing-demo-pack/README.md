# Investing Demo Pack

This demo pack is a real, openable, redacted fixture for the Furnace investing flow. It uses fictional materials only. It is not a natural 14-day or 30-day proof, and it does not contain investment advice.

## Open the fixture

Open `demos/investing-demo-pack/vault/` as a plain markdown folder or as an Obsidian vault.

Recommended entry points:

- `vault/HOME.md`
- `vault/README.md`
- `vault/output/reports/demo-investing-report.md`
- `COMPLIANCE.md`

## 10-minute walkthrough in 5 acts

1. **Act 1 - Raw intake**
   - Open `vault/raw/inbox/company-cycle-v1.md`.
   - Then open `vault/raw/inbox/industry-competition-v1.md` and `vault/raw/inbox/counter-risk-v2.md`.
   - Explain that all company names, channels, numbers, and dates are fictional or coarsened for demo use.
2. **Act 2 - Source provenance**
   - Open `vault/wiki/sources/company-cycle-v1.md`.
   - Show the `source_files`, `provenance`, and source record sections.
   - Repeat briefly for the industry and risk source pages.
3. **Act 3 - Thesis judgment**
   - Open `vault/wiki/judgments/example-semiconductor-a-thesis.md`.
   - Point to the v1 thesis, catalyst, risk, invalidation, and review cadence.
4. **Act 4 - Cross-cycle revisit**
   - In the same judgment, jump to "Cycle Review Trail".
   - Show how v2 risk evidence weakens one assumption without pretending the thesis is a 14/30-day pass.
5. **Act 5 - Knowledge compounding**
   - Open `vault/wiki/elixirs/example-semiconductor-a-supply-cycle-candidate.md`.
   - Show `derived_from`, then open `vault/output/reports/demo-investing-report.md` and one receipt under `vault/output/control/execution-receipts/`.

## What this pack demonstrates

- `raw/` keeps fictional redacted input material.
- `wiki/sources/` preserves provenance and source-page traceability.
- `wiki/judgments/` records a thesis with catalyst, risks, invalidation, and cross-cycle review.
- `wiki/elixirs/` contains a candidate reusable judgment asset derived from source and judgment pages.
- `output/reports/` and `output/control/execution-receipts/` provide reviewable outputs and audit receipts.
