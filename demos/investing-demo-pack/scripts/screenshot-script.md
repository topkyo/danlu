# Screenshot Script

Use this script to capture a short static walkthrough. Open `demos/investing-demo-pack/vault/` as a markdown folder or Obsidian vault.

## Shot list

1. **Vault home**
   - Path: `vault/HOME.md`
   - Show the pack map and compliance reminder.
2. **Raw fictional material**
   - Path: `vault/raw/inbox/company-cycle-v1.md`
   - Show the fictional company-cycle note and redaction banner.
3. **Source provenance**
   - Path: `vault/wiki/sources/company-cycle-v1.md`
   - Capture frontmatter and "Source Record".
4. **Judgment with thesis/risk/invalidation**
   - Path: `vault/wiki/judgments/example-semiconductor-a-thesis.md`
   - Capture "V1 Thesis", "Catalysts", "Risks", and "Invalidation".
5. **Cross-cycle revisit**
   - Same judgment page.
   - Capture "Cycle Review Trail" showing v1 and v2.
6. **Candidate elixir**
   - Path: `vault/wiki/elixirs/example-semiconductor-a-supply-cycle-candidate.md`
   - Capture `derived_from` and the reuse rule.
7. **Demo report**
   - Path: `vault/output/reports/demo-investing-report.md`
   - Capture conclusion boundaries and no-advice language.
8. **Execution receipt**
   - Path: `vault/output/control/execution-receipts/2026-07-14T0715Z-demo-investing-compile.json`
   - Capture `demo_fixture`, `status`, `operation`, and input/output paths.
9. **Compliance close**
   - Path: `COMPLIANCE.md`
   - Capture "No fake 14/30-day proof" and "Local-first is not offline-only".

## On-screen narration rules

- Say "fictional fixture" when showing raw material.
- Say "candidate elixir" unless the page itself says settled.
- Do not say "passed 14 days", "passed 30 days", "alpha", "signal", or "recommendation".
